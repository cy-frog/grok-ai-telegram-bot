Grok AI Telegram Bot Pricing

Use the bot for free every day. If needed, top up your balance and extend usage without a fixed monthly subscription.

Current model

Plan What is included Best for
Free access 5 Quick requests daily after channel subscription. Testing and occasional use.
Quick 1 token per request. Daily short chats and simple tasks.
Image generation 10 tokens per request. Generating images from text prompts.
Top-up via Stars Tokens are purchased as needed via Telegram Stars. Flexible usage without monthly commitment.

Top-up packs

Current packs from the bot payment panel:

  • 100 tokens -> 25 Stars
  • 500 tokens -> 125 Stars
  • 1000 tokens -> 200 Stars (-20%)
  • 5000 tokens -> 900 Stars (-28%)

Screenshot of current top-up packs in the bot.

When pay-as-you-go is cheaper

  • You ask fewer but more valuable questions, not every day.
  • Your usage is periodic (sprints, exams, launches).
  • You want strict cost control per request instead of fixed monthly spend.
  • For most users, this model is cheaper than monthly subscriptions.
