Why this bot exists

We built what Pavel Durov and Elon Musk talked about but never released: a real Grok AI inside Telegram that remembers your conversation, responds naturally, and speaks without filters.

What you get

  • Direct access to Grok inside Telegram.
  • The bot understands images: send pictures and get a response.
  • Image generation mode.
  • Video generation mode is now available.
  • Free requests refreshed daily.
  • Short context memory for coherent dialogue.
  • Balance top-up via Telegram Stars.

How to start

  1. Open the bot and send /start.
  2. Pick interface language from 11 available options.
  3. Subscribe to our group to stay informed about updates and changes.
  4. Send your question and get a Grok response.
