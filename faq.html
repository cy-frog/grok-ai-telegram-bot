Frequently Asked Questions

  1. 1. Is this an official Grok bot in Telegram?

    No. This is an independent bot that uses the official Grok API. There is currently no official working Grok AI bot in Telegram format.

  2. 2. Is there a free tier?

    Yes. Subscribers get 10 free Quick requests per day. A limited number of free image generations is also available weekly.

  3. 3. What are Telegram Stars?

    Telegram Stars are in-app credits used for top-ups and digital purchases.

  4. 4. Why can the bot stop replying?

    Common reasons are temporary API load, quota exhaustion, Telegram delivery issues, or an invalid request.

  5. 5. Can I use languages other than English?

    Yes. The bot supports 11 interface languages and multilingual prompts.

  6. 6. Does the bot remember previous messages?

    Only the last two messages are kept as temporary context for coherence.

  7. 7. Do you store my full chat history?

    No. Full chat history is not stored as a permanent database. Only two recent messages can be temporarily added for response generation, and they are not kept as a long-term archive.

  8. 8. What account data is stored?

    Telegram user ID, balance, quota information, subscription status, and payment data.

  9. 9. Is pay-as-you-go cheaper than subscription?

    In most cases, yes. If you use the bot irregularly, this model is usually cheaper than a fixed monthly subscription.

  10. 10. How much does one Quick request cost?

    Quick mode currently costs 1 token per request.

  11. 11. What top-up packs are available?

    Packs of 100, 500, 1000, and 5000 tokens are available via Telegram Stars. Larger packs include discounts up to 28%.

  12. 12. What does New Chat do?

    It resets dialogue context, so Grok stops using previous messages in that thread.

  13. 13. Which modes are available?

    Quick, Smart, Expert, Internet, and Images. Paid modes can have separate limits and pricing.

  14. 14. Are replies plain text only?

    No. The bot supports formatted text, including lists, emphasis, and readable code blocks.

  15. 15. Are refunds possible?

    Refunds are reviewed case-by-case and depend on Telegram Stars platform rules and the specific situation.

  16. 16. Are there request limits?

    Yes. Limits can apply to maintain service stability and prevent abuse.

  17. 17. Where can I follow updates and ask questions?

    Updates are published in the project Telegram channel. Comments are open, so you can ask publicly or contact the creator directly.

  18. 18. Is this a "ChatGPT killer"?

    No. This is an independent Telegram bot that gives convenient Grok access inside Telegram.

  19. 19. Can I use this bot for free?

    Yes. There is a free daily request quota and optional balance top-up when needed.

  20. 20. Does this page reflect current bot configuration?

    Yes. Current modes, pricing, and availability are listed on the Status page.
